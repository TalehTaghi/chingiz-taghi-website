About

Chingiz Taghi

Chingiz Taghi was born on November 29, 1963. He graduated from the Azerbaijan Polytechnic Institute as an engineering technologist.

He started writing at the age of 16 — his first book was the novel Epidemic of Love (Russian: «Эпидемия любви») — but only began publishing his work in 2023.

He lives and works in Baku, writes in both Azerbaijani and Russian, and continues working on new books.
