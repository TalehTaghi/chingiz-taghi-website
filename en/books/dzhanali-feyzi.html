Janali Feyzi

Life novel

The events described in this book take place in the 1980s. A young man named Janali, a student at the Azerbaijan Polytechnic Institute, is sent to the city of Kuybyshev for his work placement. There he happens to meet a soldier from his home region named Salah, who turns out to be in serious trouble during his military service. While trying to help him, Janali meets influential and deeply kind people who, in turn, help him. A sincere friendship forms between them that lasts for many years.

This book is a novel about human relationships — the compassion of some and the ingratitude of others. It is an engaging book that reads in one sitting.
