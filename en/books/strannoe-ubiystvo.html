A Strange Murder

Detective novel

The novel A Strange Murder tells of the hard fate of a former paratrooper who served in the hottest spots of the war in Afghanistan, and who was later imprisoned on a trumped-up charge and a judicial error. By fate's decree, after returning home the former paratrooper finds himself at the center of an incredibly strange event. A murder occurs that at first glance seems perfectly clear, yet on closer examination turns out to be quite strange and hard to make sense of. The former international-duty soldier takes part in the investigation as a military expert, in the course of which it emerges that the murdered man was his friend. The investigation uncovers many interesting facts. The novel is highly substantial and reads in one sitting.
