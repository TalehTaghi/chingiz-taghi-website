Angel and Hero

Romance

Angel and Hero is a novel in six parts, about the love between two young hearts who meet in a dramatic situation. On the street, a girl is pursued by a thug, and the hero confronts him and defeats him. The attacker has no choice but to retreat. After this, the young man and the young woman fall in love and build a beautiful, sincere relationship.

The hero is a strong and courageous young man, a student at the polytechnic institute. The angel is a beautiful and sincere young woman, a student at the state university. The novel is full of interesting events and sharp, stirring moments. In the end, the angel and the hero marry and step into an independent life together.
