Stopped Time

Science fiction

The novel Stopped Time is a work of science fiction. This novel tells of humanity's encounter with representatives of other civilizations living in parallel dimensions. The novel describes how beings from higher worlds try — sometimes without success — to "get through" to people in their wish to help make the human world better. After a mysterious device is passed on to certain members of the human race, various incredible things begin to happen.
