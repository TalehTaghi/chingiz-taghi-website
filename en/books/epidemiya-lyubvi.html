Epidemic of Love

Romance

The novel Epidemic of Love follows the school life of five friends — how they live, form friendships and fall in love. They are tenth-grade students who carry their friendship through a whole decade of their young lives. The story begins the moment first love settles into the friends' young hearts — a love that is pure, sincere and selfless. The novel is full of intriguing events and various conflicts, but its central theme remains sincere friendship and a great, luminous love.
