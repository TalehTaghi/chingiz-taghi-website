The Story of One War

Historical novel

The Story of One War is a historical novel spanning the period from 1828 — when Armenian settlers first arrived in Azerbaijan — to 2023. The book tells of how the peaceful life of the Azerbaijani people changed with their arrival on our lands, and how much innocent blood was shed by them over nearly two hundred years.
