Side-by-side with the Killer

Detective novel

The detective novel Side-by-side with the Killer tells of brilliant work by the law-enforcement authorities. The events take place in the early nineties of the last century. A series of murders is committed in the capital, followed by another murder in the provinces. And during each of these crimes, the same young man is invariably found nearby. Suspicion falls on him. A sudden love that flares up between the suspect and a policewoman changes the whole picture. The novel is full of interesting events and unexpected twists.

All characters and events are fictional. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental.
